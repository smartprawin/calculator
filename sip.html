Frequently asked questions

How is SIP maturity calculated?
Each monthly investment grows at the expected annual rate compounded monthly. The maturity is the future value of this annuity plus any initial lump sum.
Are SIP returns guaranteed?
No. Equity and debt funds fluctuate with the market. This calculator assumes a constant rate for illustration only.
