Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly installment calculated?
The chit value is divided equally among all members, so each member pays that share every month for the full tenure. The tenure is the number of months the chit runs (often equal to the number of members).
What does the effective annual return mean?
When you win, you receive the prize amount (chit value minus the discount) while paying smaller, dividend-adjusted installments. The effective return annualises that cashflow into a comparable yearly rate (MIRR, assumed 6% p.a.).
Why does the last person often do best?
Under the standard rule, members who haven't won yet share each month's dividend. The last winner has collected dividends in every earlier month and still receives a prize, so their net savings are usually the highest.
