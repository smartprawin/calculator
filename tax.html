Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the old and new tax regime?
The new regime for FY 2025-26 offers lower slab rates but mostly removes deductions such as 80C and HRA. The old regime keeps those deductions but applies higher slab rates. You can pick whichever gives the lower tax.
What is the section 87A rebate?
Taxpayers with income up to 12,00,000 under the new regime or up to 5,00,000 under the old regime get a rebate that can reduce their tax liability to zero.
Which deductions can I claim in the old regime?
Common deductions include Section 80C (up to 1.5 lakh), HRA, and Section 80D (health insurance). The new regime generally does not allow these deductions.
Is this calculation for FY 2025-26?
Yes, the calculator estimates Indian income tax for FY 2025-26 under both the new and old regimes.
