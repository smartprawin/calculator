Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?
For intra-state sales the tax is split equally into CGST (central) and SGST (state). For inter-state sales the whole tax is levied as IGST (integrated).
Add GST vs Remove GST?
"Add GST" treats your amount as the base and adds tax on top. "Remove GST" treats your amount as tax-inclusive and works the tax back out to find the base.
