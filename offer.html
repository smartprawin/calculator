Frequently asked questions

What is a salary split-up?
A salary split-up (or pay structure) breaks your total CTC into components such as Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, Employer PF and Gratuity. Each has different tax and retirement implications.
Why does my in-hand pay differ from CTC?
CTC includes Employer PF and Gratuity, which are not paid to you monthly. On top of that, Employee PF, Professional Tax and Income Tax are deducted from your gross pay before you receive it.
How is Employer PF and Gratuity calculated?
Both are fixed percentages of your Basic salary: Employer PF is 12% of Basic, and Gratuity is 4.81% of Basic. They are part of CTC but paid later (PF on withdrawal, Gratuity on completion of 5 years).
Is the income-tax estimate accurate?
It uses the New Regime slabs for FY 2025-26 with the standard deduction. It ignores HRA exemption, 80C and other deductions, so treat it as a rough lower/upper bound rather than your exact liability.
