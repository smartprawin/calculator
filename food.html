Frequently asked questions

How are my daily calorie needs calculated?
We estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiply by your activity level to get maintenance (TDEE). The target is then adjusted by your goal: roughly −500 kcal to lose, 0 to maintain, and +300 kcal to gain.
How are the macros split?
Protein is set at ~1.8 g per kg of body weight. Fat is 25% of target calories and carbohydrates fill the remainder. Fiber, water, added sugar and saturated fat are derived from common dietary guidelines.
Is the food calorie list exact?
The built-in values are typical per-100g figures for common Indian foods (191 items covering staples, curries, snacks, sweets, dairy, fruits, and more) sourced from IFCT 2017 and USDA data. Use "Custom" to enter an exact label value when available.
