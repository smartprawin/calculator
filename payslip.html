Frequently asked questions

Why compare my offer letter with my payslip?
The annual split-up in your offer and the monthly figures in your payslip should agree. Comparing them catches mistakes — e.g. a lower Basic, a missing PF contribution, or an in-hand amount below what was promised.
How are annual and monthly figures matched?
Each annual offer component is divided by 12 and compared with the corresponding monthly payslip value. The Stated Monthly In-Hand from the offer is compared directly with the Net Paid on the payslip.
What if a component is missing on one side?
That row shows N/A and is not treated as a pass or fail — only components present on both sides are checked for mismatch.
