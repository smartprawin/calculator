Privacy Policy

How we handle your information on this site

Overview

This website provides free calculators (EMI, EB electricity bill, and Indian income tax). It is a static, client-side site: the calculations run entirely in your browser. We aim to collect as little information about you as possible.

Information we do not collect

We do not require accounts, logins, or personal details. The values you enter (loan amount, interest rate, income, units consumed, etc.) are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Cookies and third-party advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. AdSense and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. These cookies help display ads that may be relevant to you.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in several ways:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads
  • Google Analytics opt-out: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • About Ads (choice for multiple vendors): https://www.aboutads.info/choices

You can also disable cookies through your browser settings; note this may affect how ads are displayed.

Analytics

This site does not use first-party analytics that identify you. Aggregated, anonymous usage may be measured by our hosting provider or advertising partner for operational purposes only.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the project repository or the site's contact channel.
